Every listing carries its own source URL and dates. Clinical listings do not imply endorsement or quality ranking. Imports cover the observed public sources, not every possible Northeast service. Western Mass Intergroup's meeting server returned HTTP 508 during this release; its official website remains linked. Additional fellowships and unlisted services remain outside current coverage. Counts represent listings, including separate programs and meetings at shared addresses.

## Insurance evidence

- **Facility-reported:** facility says it accepts an insurer; the record does not establish network participation under a particular product.
- **Published source:** a dated policy or plan document supports the listed wording and restrictions. It is not personalized benefits verification.
- **Directly confirmed:** requires confirmation for the listed service, provider/location, product/network and date. Record who/what was checked in the notes without private patient data.

Do not copy a hospital-level policy onto each individual clinician. Preserve exclusions. Medicare, Medicaid/MassHealth, commercial acceptance, in-network participation, eligibility and authorization are separate facts.

## Location and schedule

US Census address matches identify a street position, not a verified building entrance. Unmatched addresses fall back to a covered-state ZIP/city area only when supported by postal data, with an explicit approximate label. NPPES practice addresses are self-reported public professional data and can be stale; the registry does not establish current licensure or clinical suitability.

Online-only meetings have no coordinates. Hybrid meetings keep one identity with both formats. A recurring schedule is calculated in `America/New_York` and shown in the visitor's zone; source changes/cancellations are not predicted. Do not infer weekly days from a day-filtered index.

## Refresh workflow

1. Review a source's access and reuse requirements.
2. Fetch using a permitted interface; retain stable source IDs and provenance.
3. Normalize, validate and compare with existing records.
4. Flag changes, potential duplicates, closures and missing fields for review.
5. Publish reviewed facts, preserving audit history and each field's evidence.

The current production job checks source-link reachability only. A working URL is not clinical or meeting verification. HTTP 403 means manual review, not closure. The administrator can import reviewed JSON; insert-only imports never overwrite manually maintained records.

## Northeast expansion reviewed October 26, 2026

The directory covers CT, MA, ME, NH, NJ, NY, PA, RI and VT. The repository retains its original Massachusetts name and public domain. Counts in `public/coverage.json` are listings, not distinct venues. Multiple clinicians, programs and scheduled meetings can share an address. The grouped map represents all matching mapped records independently of list pagination.

Source collection uses only public endpoints and links embedded in official pages. It does not bypass authentication or CAPTCHAs. Cached raw exports stay outside Git. Do not copy meeting passcodes or personal contacts into the directory. Per-source update dates remain distinct from our review date. Recheck source access and reuse requirements before each refresh.
